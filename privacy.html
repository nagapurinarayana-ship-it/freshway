Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy describes information FreshWay processes when you browse products, place local-delivery orders, manage your account, or contact the business. The operator must review it against the live configuration and applicable law before relying on it for a store or app-store submission.

Information we process

  • Account name, registered mobile number, authentication data and session security information.
  • Delivery recipient/contact details, addresses, landmarks and delivery instructions.
  • Order contents, quantities, totals, status, delivery details and support history.
  • Invoice and receipt records that may be needed for accounting, tax or dispute purposes.
  • Notification subscriptions and preferences when notifications or optional WhatsApp updates are enabled.
  • Technical and security information used to operate the service, limit abuse and protect accounts.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate customers, accept and fulfil orders, coordinate delivery, provide support, prepare transaction documents, send requested updates, maintain service security and meet applicable legal obligations. Optional messaging should not be treated as mandatory unless the app clearly explains otherwise.

Service providers and sharing

The application architecture uses Cloudflare-hosted application and database services. SMS verification, web push and WhatsApp integrations may be enabled depending on production configuration. Providers process relevant information when needed to deliver an enabled feature. Information may also be shared with delivery personnel to fulfil an order and with authorities where legally required. The operator must keep this description aligned with actual production settings and provider terms.

Retention and deletion

FreshWay keeps information as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, protect the service and meet legal obligations. When an account-deletion request is completed, the account profile and operational delivery details are redacted and login credentials are removed. Issued invoice buyer snapshots are currently preserved unchanged; the operator must confirm the lawful retention basis and publish the applicable retention period before store submission. Other records may be retained only as required for accounting, tax, security or dispute purposes, with use restricted where appropriate.

Submit a request from Profile in the app or use the external account-deletion request page. Requests are reviewed; linked orders and invoices are not immediately erased.

Security

FreshWay uses authentication controls, protected sessions, access controls and rate limits where implemented. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Contact FreshWay if you suspect unauthorised use of your account.

Children and international processing

FreshWay is intended for customers able to place orders and manage an account. Contact FreshWay if a child has provided personal information. Service providers may process data in other locations; the operator should confirm provider locations and applicable safeguards.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. For questions or to follow up on a request, open FreshWay and use Contact Us in Profile. Before app-store submission, the operator must verify and publish the legal business identity, a monitored privacy contact, retention periods and all actual production data flows.

Operator review required: This initial policy needs business/legal review and a production data-flow check before final Play Store submission.
