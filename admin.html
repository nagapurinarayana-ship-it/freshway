BILLING & TAX

Invoice configuration

The receipt uses saved order and Store Profile details. The tax number is optional: when present it is printed as “Tax Number”; when blank that line is omitted. No external/background GSTIN verification is performed for commercial receipts, and GST is not added to their saved order total. Seller details remain editable below.

Commercial receipt note: The tax number is optional display text for commercial receipts. It is not checked against an external service and does not trigger GST calculations.
Invoice issuance is disabled until configuration is saved.

This also checks existing Out for Delivery and Delivered orders. New orders receive an invoice when dispatched. Refunded orders are excluded because they need separate credit-note review.
